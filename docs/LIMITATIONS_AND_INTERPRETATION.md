# Limitations and Interpretation

## Main Limitations

- Landsat's 30-metre resolution limits individual-building detection.
- Population rasters are modelled estimates rather than direct settlement census counts.
- Seasonal vegetation and image-acquisition differences may affect spectral comparisons.
- The 13 validation settlements are selected cases and not a complete census of abandoned
  settlements in Borno State.
- Remote sensing cannot independently establish the precise social or conflict-related cause
  of every observed physical change.
- Independent field confirmation was unavailable for the final classifications.

## Interpretation Rule

The classifications represent the strength of available geospatial evidence.

A probable-abandonment classification should not be interpreted as legal or field-confirmed
proof of permanent settlement abandonment.

## Recommended Verification

Highest-ranked settlements should be prioritised for:

- field assessment;
- high-resolution image review;
- humanitarian-record comparison;
- local-government verification;
- community-based confirmation.
