# Data and Methods

## Data Sources

### WorldPop

WorldPop population estimates were used to assess settlement-area population patterns for
2015 and 2023.

### Landsat

Paired Landsat imagery supported visual and spectral comparison of settlement conditions.

### Spectral Indices

NDVI was used to assess vegetation behaviour, while NDBI supported interpretation of built-up
surface change.

### Settlement Locations

A harmonised database containing 2,455 settlement locations was used for statewide screening.

### Administrative Boundaries

Borno State and Local Government Area boundaries supported settlement assignment, aggregation
and final cartographic presentation.

## Analytical Workflow

1. Prepare and harmonise settlement records.
2. Assign settlements to LGAs.
3. integrate 2015 and 2023 population estimates.
4. calculate population-change indicators.
5. calculate built-up change indicators.
6. screen candidate settlements statistically.
7. prepare Landsat image pairs.
8. compare RGB, NDVI and NDBI evidence.
9. conduct structured manual interpretation.
10. assign final classifications and confidence levels.
11. rank evidence strength.
12. produce final maps, charts, tables and reports.

## Final Classification Classes

- Confirmed abandonment
- Probable abandonment
- Possible abandonment
- Uncertain
- Not supported
- Reconstruction or reoccupation

## Quality Assurance

Quality assurance included:

- unique validation identifiers;
- record-count checks;
- coordinate-completeness checks;
- boundary-containment validation;
- image-pair checks;
- structured manual adjudication;
- final result-register validation;
- map and figure registers;
- package-level validation.

## Reproducibility

Some source datasets may not be redistributed because of licensing, file size or access
restrictions. Final processed outputs and documentation are included where appropriate.
