# Limitations

This project uses multiple datasets because each source measures a different part of settlement change.

The main limitations are:

1. **Population data are modelled.**  
   WorldPop estimates population distribution but does not directly confirm whether a particular settlement is occupied.

2. **Night-time lights measure detected light, not population.**  
   Changes can also reflect electricity access, settlement size, cloud/sensor effects or changes in local activity.

3. **Built-up change does not directly measure occupancy.**  
   A decline in the built signal does not establish whether buildings are damaged, demolished, vacant or temporarily unused.

4. **Conflict exposure does not establish causation.**  
   A settlement may be close to recorded events, but spatial proximity alone does not prove that a specific event caused the observed change.

5. **The 13 detailed cases are not a representative sample of all settlements in Borno State.**  
   They were retained because their evidence profiles required closer interpretation.

6. **Abandonment cannot be confirmed from these datasets alone.**  
   Direct field evidence, reliable occupancy records or another independent source would still be required.

For these reasons, the repository uses terms such as **settlement contraction**, **change signal** and **priority case** instead of treating the 13 locations as confirmed abandoned settlements.
