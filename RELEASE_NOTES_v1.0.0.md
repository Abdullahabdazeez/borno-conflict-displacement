## First prepared release

This repository presents an integrated geospatial assessment of conflict-induced displacement and settlement abandonment across Borno State, Nigeria, between 2015 and 2023.

### Included

- Screening of 2,455 settlements across 27 LGAs
- Settlement-level population and built-environment change evidence
- Conflict-exposure and displacement-context assessment
- Landsat spectral and visual validation
- Multi-indicator convergence and settlement typology
- Manual evidence adjudication
- Final settlement evidence classification
- LGA-level concentration analysis
- Four final maps and five publication figures
- Settlement, decision-distribution and LGA result tables
- Selected validation registers
- Results-review notebook
- Result-reproduction and repository-validation scripts
- Methodology, limitations and project-closure documentation

### Principal findings

- 13 candidate settlements across six LGAs received detailed validation.
- 5 settlements were classified as probable abandonment.
- 1 settlement was classified as possible abandonment.
- 4 settlements remained uncertain.
- 2 cases were not supported as abandonment.
- 1 settlement showed reconstruction or reoccupation.
- No settlement was classified as confirmed abandonment.
- Wumbi was the highest-ranked settlement.
- Kala Balge was the highest-ranked LGA.

### Interpretation

The final classes represent evidence strength rather than absolute proof. Statistical decline alone was insufficient to establish abandonment, so the final decisions were based on multi-indicator convergence, visual assessment and manual adjudication.
